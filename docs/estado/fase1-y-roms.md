# Estado del juego y crónica de las ROMs

## Resumen (2026-11-05)

- Arranque: Nintendo → publisher → devs → dedicatoria (no se saltan; `PERMITE_SALTO` = 0) → introducción de 3 escenas (Start o A la salta) → título (vuelve a la introducción tras 15 s sin tocar).
- Título → selector «SELECT YOUR FIGHTER» (Tarou o Bárbara) → **fase 1 del beat 'em up**: aldea (2048 px) → bosque → monasterio (escena de Rex; desde aquí la barra de apoyo: R cambia, X invoca a Lucinda, Rex o Fenrir) → dojo (kendoka, Gomasuri-dono, su escena) → STAGE CLEAR → «STAGE 2 / COMING SOON».
- Cada escenario se carga entrando otra vez en `PANTALLA_FASE1` con la vida y el arma de antes; CONTINUE reinicia el escenario. Pausa con Start (media luz), GAME OVER con cuenta atrás.
- Fondos: «frente» (faroles, barriles) al fondo del carril y sin prioridad; los objetos (rompibles, ítems, proyectiles, chispa) opacos gracias a las ventanas de color por HDMA (canal 5).
- Ritmo (2026-11-05): los golpes duran 12/16 frames (antes 14/20), los ninjas cambian de cuadro cada 3 (antes 4), su puñetazo 24 frames (28), el derribo 48 (64), el aturdimiento 12 (16); las tablas de `personaje.c` van a 2 frames por dibujo en los golpes. Los cuadros de los enemigos ya no se ven a medias (titileo): las ranuras 1 y 2 usan doble búfer cuando están solas y, si no, el cuadro va entero en un VBlank (`PRESUPUESTO_SUBIDA` 2048 B, repartido a turnos con la protagonista).
- El logo de Nintendo y el del publisher llevan el negro en el color 0 (transparente) de su paleta: antes el blanco caía ahí y las letras se veían negras (`fondo_a_cero` en `componer_escenas.py`).
- El plataformas (nivel 1, tutorial) sigue compilado, solo se llega con `-DGUION_PRUEBA`; congelado en el tag `plataformas-r2`.
- Pendiente: el relevo con L (sin decidir), arte flojo o de sustitución (Tarou: aéreo, agarre, golpe de agarre, lanzamiento, especial, lanzar arma; la meditación de Rex), audio, el HUD definitivo (otra sesión).

## Arranque y nivel 1 (plataformas, Revisión 2)

What the current ROM does when run (Revisión 2, 2026-10-20): the boot sequence (Nintendo → publisher → devs → dedication → 3-scene intro (since 2026-11-05 Start or A skips it to the title; the logos and the dedication still can't be skipped, `PERMITE_SALTO` stays 0) → title with parallax; the title appears at ~47 s and loops to the intro after 15 s without input; the Nintendo logo is animated by the scene engine: mosaic focus-in, a diagonal light shine, fade out), then Start on the title enters **level 1, a movement tutorial** of 2048 px (8 screens) with collision: Tarou (adult husky, the real Pixellab art in 96x64 cells; the attack is a fixed pose) walks, runs (Y), jumps with variable height (B), crouches and crouch-walks (Down), unsheathes/sheathes the sword (R), attacks (A) and sleeps after 10 s idle, and each obstacle needs one of those moves: 16/32 px steps, a 48 px wall (hold B), a 48 px tunnel (crouch; you can't stand up under it), a 96 px pit (run and jump), one-way platforms over thorns, a log that only the sword breaks, then the exit → "LEVEL 2 / COMING SOON" → title. Falling or touching thorns respawns at the last sign (each sign is a checkpoint). Signs, thorns and log are placeholder art. SELECT toggles the debug HUD (8 rows; since 2026-10-21 it costs ~6 frames over the whole playthrough, measured with the self-playing test ROM; respawn also verified there); START returns to the title. Design: `docs/diseno-tutorial-movimiento.md` (Revisión 2) on top of `docs/diseno-demo-jugable.md` (Revisión 1). Keyboard mapping of Mesen on this machine: arrows, A=S, B=A, X=X, Y=Z, L=Q, R=W, Start=D, Select=E (`docs/mesen-y-depuracion.md`). The capture script forces `EmulationSpeed=100` and `AllowBackgroundInput=true` on the command line; neither is saved to the author's settings. The scripted keys can still be lost (Mesen's focus jumps to its menu bar) while the author uses the PC: the reliable condition is to leave the PC alone during a run; the effect of `AllowBackgroundInput` is not proven.

## ROM de prueba de carga

Load-test ROM for the beat 'em up (`docs/diseno-beat-em-up.md` §9, results in §9.1 and §9.2): `src/pantallas/prueba_carga.c` runs 8 configurations of 6 s each with no input (Tarou + up to 3 ninjas of 64x96 on the new sprite layer), and the HUD shows the per-config maxima. The beat 'em up sprite layer it exercises: `scripts/piezas16.py` (PNG strip → `.p16`: 16x16 pieces without the empty ones; `--rejilla-optima` shifts each frame's 16x16 grid by 0–15 px to the offset with fewest pieces, used for Tarou since 2026-11-04, index + piece table + mirrored table + tiles in upload order; the Makefile builds Tarou's 14 strips this way), `src/juego/p16.c` and `subida.c` (pure: frame index and the upload plan in 512 B rows), `dma_copias.asm`, `sprites.c` + `sprites_oam.asm` (fixed entity table, one asm call per frame writes the whole OAM; fast path of one 16-bit add per piece). Not used by the platformer level yet. Its ninja assets are versioned `.bin` files in `res/prueba_carga/`; `python scripts/preparar_prueba_carga.py` regenerates them (it needs the FULL_Samurai pack in `res/descargas/`) and prints the per-line sprite-slot predictions. ## La fase 1 entera (los «8 puntos» del 2026-11-05)

The whole stage 1 (2026-11-05, the «8 points»): **four scenarios** loaded one after another by re-entering `PANTALLA_FASE1` (`fase1_escenario`, `fase1_vida`, `fase1_arma` in `pantalla_fase1.c`, declared in `pantallas.h`; `pantalla_fase1_desde_inicio()` from the selector): aldea (2048 px) → bosque → monasterio → dojo (1024 px each, `scripts/preparar_zona.py` → `res/zonas/`, `fondo_nivel_cargar_escenario`, per-scenario far layer and HDMA sky; noren and smoke only in the village). `oleadas.c` has a table per scenario (points, width, enemy types per wave via `ninja_oleada_tipos`); after the last wave the player walks to the right edge (`SALIDA_MARGEN`) and the next scenario loads with the same life and weapon; CONTINUE restarts the current scenario. Monastery: Rex's text scene (`juego/dialogo.c`, world frozen, rows 6–7) and from there the **support bar** (`juego/apoyo.c`, `hud/hud_apoyo.c` in row 5): hits fill it, R cycles Lucinda/Rex/Fenrir, X invokes; the support is drawn in the protagonist's place (`protagonista_sprite_cambiar`, palette 0 swapped in VBlank) while the world freezes; Rex = mosaic, Fenrir = shake. Dojo: kendoka, then Gomasuri-dono; after him his text scene, Tarou's victory pose and STAGE CLEAR. Breakable objects use sprite palette 7, which is inside the scene's half-add colour math, so they looked like ghosts; since 2026-11-05 `objetos_sprite.c` puts colour windows 1 and 2 around what is DRAWN of every object on screen (breakables, floor item, projectiles, splinters, hit spark; tight boxes `OBJ_CAJA_*` written by `preparar_objetos.py`; where more than two share lines the closest ranges are merged), drawn per line band by HDMA channel 5 (mode 4, WH0..WH3, double-buffered table swapped in `objetos_sprite_vblank`), with `CGWSEL = OBJETOS_CGWSEL` (0x22: colour math only outside the windows); the shadow, smoke and birds stay translucent and the pause dimming (CGWSEL 0) still covers everything. Tarou's beat 'em up strips now include `golpe_4`, `levantarse`, `derribado`, `victoria` (`preparar_personaje.py tarou --extras`). Test ROMs: `-DESCENARIO_INICIAL=n` boots into scenario n; `-DGUION_APOYO` keeps the bar full and presses R/X from the script.
